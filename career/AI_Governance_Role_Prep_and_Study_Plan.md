## 3. Your positioning line for this role

There is a genuinely current insight here that will land with anyone who works in this space:

> **Process mining tells you what to automate. Agentic AI executes it.** Most organisations have one side —
> a Celonis instance producing findings nobody actions, or GenAI pilots nobody can justify. I work from both
> ends: I have spent 14 years finding and quantifying process bottlenecks in regulated banking, and in the
> last year I have built seven agentic and RAG systems that actually do the work at the other end. The
> discipline that connects them is governance — a use-case portfolio triaged by value and risk, a business
> case with a measured baseline, and an EU AI Act position agreed before the build rather than after.

This is not a stretch. Celonis launched Agent Mining and extended its Orchestration Engine to coordinate AI
agents alongside people and systems in 2026, and the platform now ships an MCP server to feed process
context to agents. The two disciplines are converging, and you sit on both sides of the convergence.

**Second line, for the compliance bullet:**

> "The EU AI Act work isn't a separate workstream for me — it's an input to prioritisation. A high-risk
> classification changes the effort estimate by a factor of three, so it belongs in the scoring model at
> intake, not in a compliance review at the end. That's how I built the triage model at Capgemini and it's
> what stops a portfolio filling up with use cases that can't ship."

## 4. The four gaps and how to close them

### Gap 1 — BPM tooling: Celonis and SAP Signavio *(highest priority, and free)*

**Celonis Academy is free.** 300+ online courses, and the courses and Partner Certification Exams are
available in German as well as English. The Process Mining Fundamentals track is about 9 hours; qualification
exams give you a **certification digital badge** you can put on LinkedIn.

- [Celonis Academy](https://academy.celonis.com/) — start here
- [Process Mining Fundamentals learning path](https://academy.celonis.com/learning-paths/process-mining-fundamentals-for-students) (~9h)
- [Process Mining: From Theory to Execution](https://academy.celonis.com/learning-paths/process-mining-from-theory-to-execution)
- [Introduction to Process Mining](https://academy.celonis.com/courses/introduction-to-process-mining)
- [Celonis training overview](https://www.celonis.com/training)
- Context on the convergence: [What is AI-enhanced process mining](https://www.celonis.com/blog/ai-enhanced-process-mining) · [AI process discovery](https://www.celonis.com/blog/ai-process-discovery)

**SAP Signavio** — you already have SAP S/4HANA on your CV, which helps. Free learning first, paid
certification only if a specific employer requires it.

- [SAP Learning: Managing Business Processes with SAP Signavio](https://learning.sap.com/courses/managing-business-processes-with-sap-signavio-solutions) — free
- [SIG121 — Introduction to BPMN 2.0 and SAP Signavio Process Manager](https://app30.training.sap.com/course/sig121-fundamentals-of-bpmn-20-classroom-001-g-en/) — the official paid course
- [Signavio BPMN training](https://www.signavio.com/services-and-support/training-coaching/bpmn-training/) — available in German
- Formal cert: SAP Certified Process Management Consultant (C_SIGPM), 120 min, 55% pass — only if required

### Gap 2 — BPMN 2.0 *(a weekend, essentially free)*

BPMN 2.0 is a notation, not a discipline. You can be genuinely competent in a weekend and it closes an
explicit JD keyword.

- [BPMN 2.0 for efficient process design — SAP Signavio](https://www.signavio.com/bpmn-2-0-for-efficient-process-design/)
- [bpmn.io](https://bpmn.io/) — **free open-source modeller, no signup.** Use this for the deliverable below.
- [Camunda Modeler](https://camunda.com/download/modeler/) — free desktop tool
- [OMG BPMN specification](https://www.omg.org/spec/BPMN/2.0/) — reference, skim only

### Gap 3 — Lean Six Sigma *(~$200–440 if you self-study)*

You have done the work without the vocabulary. Green Belt is the right level — Black Belt is
over-investment for an AI leadership role.

| Body | Exam cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **IASSC** | ~$195–350 exam voucher | **Self-study friendly** — no mandatory training. Best value. Renewal ~$175. |
| **ASQ** | $338 member / $438 non-member | Most recognised in the US; strong globally |
| Training bundles | $1,500–5,000 | **Skip.** You do not need to be taught process improvement. |

- [IASSC](https://iassc.org/) · [ASQ Six Sigma Green Belt](https://asq.org/cert/six-sigma-green-belt)
- Free study: [ASQ Six Sigma resources](https://asq.org/quality-resources/six-sigma) · DMAIC, VSM, SIPOC, control charts

**Frame it correctly on your CV:** you are not learning process improvement — you are certifying what you
already did. "Forecast cycle 5 days → 8 hours" *is* a DMAIC project.

### Gap 4 — RPA and facilitation vocabulary *(free)*

- **RPA:** [Microsoft Learn — Power Automate](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/browse/?products=power-automate) (free) or [UiPath Academy](https://academy.uipath.com/) (free). You need enough to say where RPA stops and where an agent starts — that boundary is a good interview answer, not a tooling exercise.
- **Design Thinking:** [IDEO U free resources](https://www.ideou.com/) · [SAP's design thinking material](https://www.sap.com/design/)

## 7. Interview questions specific to this role

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| **"Have you used Celonis or Signavio?"** | Do not bluff. *"I've completed the Celonis Process Mining Fundamentals track and hold the qualification badge — I'm competent on the concepts and the tool, not battle-tested on a large deployment. What I do bring is 14 years of finding and quantifying the bottlenecks that process mining surfaces, and the ability to turn a finding into a funded, governed AI initiative. In my experience the tool isn't the constraint — acting on what it shows you is."* |
| **"Walk me through a process you transformed."** | Standard Chartered: as-is regulatory forecast cycle, 50+ stakeholders, 5 days. Where the time actually went. To-be design. 8 hours. Then the second-order benefit: shift-left gates cut calculation errors 85%. **Have the BPMN model on screen.** |
| **"How do you identify AI use cases across departments?"** | The intake funnel: three sources (process mining, workshops, intake form), the scoring model with the EU AI Act risk multiplier, and the number that matters — 23 candidates → 6 funded. Explain why the executive favourite scored sixth. |
| **"How do you know an AI initiative delivered value?"** | Baseline captured **before** go-live, measured from the system of record not self-reported, signed off quarterly by Finance, and the AI function does not certify its own benefits. Then the honest part: at AEGIS I refused to put improved financial-crime detection in the business case because I couldn't evidence it. |
| **"Six Sigma background?"** | *"No belt yet — I'm sitting the IASSC Green Belt. But the work is there: cutting a forecast cycle from five days to eight hours was a DMAIC project in everything but the name. I'm certifying what I've done rather than learning it."* |
| **"How do you work with Legal and IT Security on the EU AI Act?"** | Risk classification is a **second-line decision, not mine** — I own intake and release, they own classification and can veto at any gate. Then the practical point: classification belongs at intake because a high-risk classification triples the effort estimate. |
| **"RPA experience?"** | Give the boundary, not a tool list. RPA for deterministic, high-volume, stable-interface steps; agents for conditional work where the next step depends on the last result. Then: most failed automation programmes used RPA where the process wasn't stable enough. |
| **"How do you drive adoption?"** | Trust, not training. The AEGIS shadow-mode story: four weeks of the system running silently alongside analysts before anyone was asked to rely on it. Adoption is built with evidence over time, not with a launch. |
| **"What would you do in your first 90 days?"** | Inventory and classify the existing AI estate first — you cannot govern what you cannot enumerate. Then intake funnel and scoring model. Then one use case chosen partly for the reusable infrastructure it forces you to build. |
